# Normal Restaurant Website: Problems and Our Solution

Why most Indian restaurants and cafés skip a website (or regret the one they have), and how our platform answers each problem. Use this for sales pitches, the marketing website and feature priorities.

- **Last updated:** 2026-10-10
- **Context:** about 13% of listed restaurants in India have a website (Rentech SmartScraper listing data, April 2026). Owners rely on Zomato/Swiggy, Google Maps and Instagram instead.

Phases refer to [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md).

---

## 1. Getting customers

| # | Problem with a normal website | What we make | Phase |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Nobody visits it; it brings no customers by itself | QR code on every table and parcel, link in the Google Business Profile and Instagram bio, WhatsApp offers to past customers, so traffic comes from the restaurant's own customers | 8 |
| 2 | Customers already order on Zomato/Swiggy and see no reason to switch | Direct-order price or perk (lower price, free item, loyalty points) that the 25–35% commission saving pays for | 8 |
| 3 | Not found on Google | Each site set up with the restaurant's Google Business Profile, menu pages Google can read, and correct name, address and phone | 8 |
| 4 | No way to bring customers back | Customer list owned by the restaurant (phone, order history) with WhatsApp offers and loyalty points | 8 |

## 2. Keeping it up to date

| # | Problem with a normal website | What we make | Phase |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | Menu and prices go out of date because changes need a developer | One menu in the Business panel feeds the POS, QR menu, website and app; a change shows everywhere at once | 1, 8 |
| 6 | Items show as available when they're sold out | Item on/off switch from the POS or kitchen screen, reflected live online | 3, 8 |
| 7 | Wrong opening hours and holiday timings | Hours and holidays set once in the Business panel; online ordering closes automatically outside them | 1, 8 |
| 8 | Old photos and a dated look | 10 modern templates with a theme editor; switching templates keeps the menu and photos | 8 |
| 9 | The freelancer who built it disappeared, the domain or hosting expired | Hosting, domain, SSL and updates included in the monthly plan; one platform keeps every site updated | 2, 8 |

## 3. Taking orders

| # | Problem with a normal website | What we make | Phase |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | It's only a brochure; customers can't order or book | Online ordering, table booking and QR table ordering built in | 8 |
| 11 | Orders arrive by phone or WhatsApp and get missed or written wrong | Every order goes straight to the kitchen screen and POS, with sound alerts and status tracking | 3, 8 |
| 12 | No online payment, or a payment link sent by hand | Razorpay/UPI checkout, pay at counter and cash on delivery | 8 |
| 13 | Orders on the website and in the shop are kept in separate places | Website, QR, waiter, POS and phone orders land in one system with one sales report | 3, 8 |
| 14 | No GST bill for online orders | GST invoice generated for every order, online or in-store | 3 |

## 4. Delivery

| # | Problem with a normal website | What we make | Phase |
|---|---|---|---|
| 15 | Website takes delivery orders but the restaurant has no delivery system | Delivery app for the restaurant's own riders, plus one-tap booking of third-party delivery partners when own riders are busy or too far | 5 |
| 16 | Customer keeps calling to ask where the order is | Live order status and rider location for the customer; WhatsApp updates | 5, 8 |
| 17 | Cash on delivery is hard to track | Cash collected per rider recorded and settled at day close | 5 |
| 18 | Delivery is late or fails, leading to bad reviews | Order timing on the kitchen screen, proof of delivery (OTP or photo), rider performance reports | 3, 5 |

## 5. Cost and effort

| # | Problem with a normal website | What we make | Phase |
|---|---|---|---|
| 19 | ₹20,000–1,00,000 upfront for a site that may not work | Low setup fee plus a monthly plan; 14-day free trial before paying | 2 |

## 6. Trust and safety

| # | Problem with a normal website | What we make | Phase |
|---|---|---|---|
| 23 | Customers don't trust paying on an unknown site | HTTPS everywhere, known payment gateway, clear refund and privacy pages per restaurant | 2, 8 |
| 24 | Fake or copied websites and old domains confuse customers | Verified restaurant profile; one official link used in QR codes, Google and Instagram | 8 |
| 25 | Customer data sits in a freelancer's spreadsheet or a WhatsApp chat | Data stored securely per restaurant; only the restaurant's own staff can see it | 1 |

## Pitch summary (one line per problem group)

1. **Getting customers:** "Your site gets traffic from your own tables, Google and WhatsApp, not from hope."
2. **Keeping it updated:** "Change the menu once; the POS, QR menu, website and app all update."
3. **Taking orders:** "Every order, from any channel, goes straight to your kitchen and bill."
4. **Delivery:** "Your own riders, or a partner rider in one tap, tracked live, with no 25–35% commission."
5. **Cost and effort:** "One monthly plan replaces four tools, and we set it up for you."
6. **Trust:** "Secure payments, proper policies and one official link."
