# Go-to-Market Plan

Where and how to sell the restaurant platform. Phases refer to [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md); leads are tracked in the Sales CRM (Phase 2).

- **Last updated:** 2026-10-10

---

## Who to sell to

| Priority | Segment | Why | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Independent cafés (20–60 seats) | Need POS + waiter + kitchen; owner decides alone; open to new tech | From the pilot (Phase 4) |
| 2 | Family restaurants (single outlet) | Daily billing pain; many on paper or old billing software | From the pilot |
| 3 | Cloud kitchens and QSRs | Heavy delivery volume; feel aggregator commission most | After Phase 5 (delivery) |
| 4 | Small chains (2–5 outlets) | Bigger contracts; need central menu and reports | After Phase 6 |
| Later | Office canteens, colleges | Only after the DeskDyne agreement check | Phase 7 |
| Avoid early | Big brands, fine-dine chains | Already on Petpooja/Restroworks; long sales cycles | — |

## Where

- **Months 1–6: Pune only**, area by area: Baner/Balewadi → Koregaon Park/Kalyani Nagar → Viman Nagar → Aundh → Kothrud → Wakad/Hinjewadi → FC Road/JM Road.
- **Months 6–12:** Mumbai (Thane, Navi Mumbai first), then Nashik and Nagpur.
- **Year 2:** Bengaluru, Hyderabad, and partner-led expansion to other cities.

## How

### Step 1: Now, while building
1. List 100 Pune cafés and restaurants (Google Sheet now; import into the Sales CRM later).
2. Talk to 20 owners. Ask: how do you bill, how do orders reach the kitchen, how much goes to Zomato/Swiggy each month?
3. Sign 3 pilot cafés: free for 3 months in return for feedback and a testimonial.

### Step 2: Pilot (Phase 4)
- Set up each café in person: menu, printer, staff training; visit weekly in the first month.
- Measure: bills per day, order mistakes, time saved, money saved.
- Goal: 2 of 3 agree to pay.

### Step 3: Sales channels
| Channel | How | Expected share |
|---|---|---|
| Direct visits | Walk in at 3–5 pm; 10-minute tablet demo; leave a brochure | ~40% |
| Billing-machine and printer dealers | 20% recurring commission per restaurant | ~25% (largest long-term) |
| Referrals | One free month per referred restaurant | ~15% |
| Instagram / YouTube Shorts | Real pilot numbers ("saved ₹18,000 on Zomato last month") | ~10% |
| Google Ads and SEO | "Restaurant billing software Pune", "QR menu Pune" | ~5% |
| Associations and expos | Pune hotel association, NRAI chapter, AAHAR | ~5% |

### Step 4: Offer
- 14-day free trial, no card.
- Setup by us: menu, printer and staff training included.
- Monthly plan, cancel anytime.
- Founding price for the first 20 restaurants, locked for 12 months: Starter ₹999 (normally ₹1,499), Pro ₹1,999 (normally ₹2,999).
- Switching from Petpooja: we move the menu over for free.

## 10-minute demo script

1. **Problem (1 min):** "How much goes to Zomato every month? How do orders reach your kitchen?"
2. **Waiter app (2 min):** take a table order on a phone.
3. **Kitchen screen (2 min):** order appears with sound, marked ready.
4. **POS (2 min):** print a GST bill, split it.
5. **Owner dashboard (2 min):** today's sales and best sellers on the owner's phone.
6. **Close (1 min):** "Free for 14 days, we set it up. Shall we start Monday?"

## Selling points

- 0% commission; the restaurant owns its customer data.
- Own delivery riders plus one-tap third-party delivery partners.
- Low price and local support in Pune.
- Proof to show: Brew & Leaf demo (`templates/modern-cafe`), corporate food-ordering work (present as Vernoka company work, keep private), WhatsApp integration experience.

## Market facts used in pitches (sources in the planning discussion)

- About 13% of listed restaurants in India have a website (Rentech SmartScraper, April 2026).
- Zomato/Swiggy effectively take 25–35% of each order after ads, discounts and GST.
- Petpooja costs about ₹15,000–30,000 a year per outlet in practice.
- Diners spend about 25% more when ordering directly from a restaurant's own site (Toast data).
