From order to bill, in one system. 0% commission.

Waiter app, kitchen screen and GST billing for cafés and restaurants. Orders reach the kitchen in seconds, bills print in one tap, and you see today's sales on your phone.

Waiter app on any phone

Tap the table, tap the dishes, “Send to kitchen”. No more running to the kitchen with paper slips, no more lost orders.

Kitchen screen with sound

New orders ring on the kitchen tablet. Cooks tap Start and Ready; the waiter sees it at once.

GST bill in one tap

CGST + SGST, round-off, discounts, packaging charge. Split the bill by people or by items. Cash, UPI and card, even mixed.

Works when the internet doesn't

Orders and bills keep working offline and sync themselves when the connection is back. Nothing is lost.

Cash that adds up

Open the day with the drawer cash, record purchases paid from the drawer, and close the day: it tells you if cash is short or extra.

Owner on WhatsApp

Today's sales, bills, cash and UPI arrive on the owner's WhatsApp at day close, wherever you are.
