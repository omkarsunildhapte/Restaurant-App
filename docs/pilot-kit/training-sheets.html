Waiter
Taking orders on your phone
Restaurant:  
Staff app:  

First time only

  1. Open the invite email from Thaliyo and set your password.
  2. Open the Staff app link in Chrome, sign in, choose your restaurant.
  3. Chrome menu ⋮ → Add to Home screen. From now on, open it from the home screen.
  4. Want Marathi or Hindi? Menu → Language.

Take an order

  1. On Tables, tap the table. Colours: Free, Ordering, Bill requested.
  2. Tap each dish. If it asks for a size or add-ons, choose, then Add · ₹…. Use Note for the kitchen for “less spicy, no onion”.
  3. Check the order: Show order ▴. Use + / − to change quantities.
  4. Tap Send to kitchen (3). The kitchen gets it at once. Each send is one round.
  5. More food later? Open the same table, add dishes, Send to kitchen again.

Know when food is ready

Open the table → Show order ▴. Each round shows Sent → Preparing → Ready → Served. When it says Ready, pick it up.

Bill

Guest asks for the bill → tap Ask for bill. The counter sees “bill asked” and prints it.

Dish greyed out? It's sold out today. Tell the guest and suggest something else.
“Offline” at the top? Keep working. Orders are saved on the phone and go to the kitchen when the internet is back. Tell the kitchen by voice if it's urgent.
Never share your password. Each person has their own login, so every order shows who took it.

Kitchen
The kitchen screen (KDS)
Restaurant:  

Start of the shift

  1. Turn on the kitchen tablet, open Thaliyo Staff from the home screen. It opens on Kitchen.
  2. Tap 🔕 Tap for sound once, so it shows 🔔 Sound on. New orders now ring.
  3. Keep the tablet on charge. In the tablet's settings, set the screen to stay on.

Each order

The screen has three columns: New → Preparing → Ready to serve. Each card is one round for one table: table name, order number, dishes with quantities and notes.

  1. A new card rings and appears under New.
  2. When you start cooking it, tap Start. It moves to Preparing.
  3. When the food is ready, tap Ready. The waiter's phone shows Ready.
  4. When the waiter takes it, tap Served. The card leaves the screen.

Read the notes on each dish (e.g. “less spicy”). The same dish ordered twice shows as 2 ×.

Something has run out

Tap Sold out at the top, find the dish, tap it so it shows Sold out. Waiters can't order it any more. When it's back, tap it again to make it Available.

“Offline” at the top? New orders can't reach the kitchen screen until the internet is back. Waiters will tell you orders by voice; the screen catches up by itself.

Cashier
Counter: billing, takeaway, day close
Restaurant:  

1 · Open the day (every morning)

Day → count the cash in the drawer → Opening cash → Open day. Bills can't be made until the day is open.

2 · Takeaway or phone order

POS → + Takeaway or + Phone order (name, phone) → tap the dishes.

3 · Bill a table

  1. On POS, tap the order in the list (tables that asked show “bill asked”).
  2. Bill · ₹…
  3. Optional: customer name and phone, Discount.
  4. Payment: All Cash / All UPI / All Card. For cash, type Cash given to see the Change.
  5. Settle & print · ₹…

Paying two ways? Split 2 and set each amount until it says Payments match the bill. Each person pays for their own dishes? By items.

4 · During the day

  • Guests changed table: open the order → Move / merge → tap a free table (move) or a busy table (merge into one bill).
  • Dish ran out: Sold out.
  • Reprint a bill: Bills → find it → Print again.
  • Wrong bill: call the manager. They Void… it in Bills, then Bill again.
  • Cash paid from the drawer (milk, vegetables, gas): Day → Type, For, Amount → Pay out.

5 · Close the day (every night)

  1. Make sure every table is billed (“No open orders”).
  2. Day → count the drawer → Counted cash.
  3. It shows Cash matches, Short ₹… or Extra ₹…. Write a note if short.
  4. Close day → Send summary on WhatsApp to the owner.
Internet gone? Keep billing. The bill prints as provisional and gets its number when the internet is back. Don't close the day while it says “to sync”.
Printer: Menu → Printing → choose the printer → Test print. Receipt paper: 58 mm or 80 mm.

Owner & manager
Business panel and staff app
Business panel:  
Staff app:  

Your two apps

Business panel (laptop or phone)

Set up and manage: menu (prices, sizes, add-ons, GST), tables, staff, outlets, settings (business details, GSTIN, round-off, packaging charge), orders, purchases, salaries and your billing (Thaliyo plan).

Staff app (counter tablet)

The same app your team uses. As owner or manager you can use every screen: Tables, Kitchen, POS, Bills (void a bill) and Day.

Add or remove staff

  1. Business panel → Staff → Invite someone: their email, role (waiter, kitchen, cashier, manager) and outlet.
  2. They get an invite email and set their own password. You never need to know it.
  3. Someone leaves? Staff → Remove, the same day. Until someone accepts, they show as “Waiting to accept”.

More Google reviews

Every bill prints a “Review us on Google” QR, and the table cards carry the same QR; your website has the button too. Ask happy guests to scan it before they leave. Set up once in the Business panel (outlet → Google Place ID).

Change the menu

Business panel → Menu: new dish, new price, a dish off the menu for good. For “just today” use Sold out in the staff app instead.

Every day

  • Morning: the cashier opens the day with the drawer cash.
  • Night: the day summary arrives on your WhatsApp: sales, bills, cash, UPI + card, paid out, short/extra.
  • Short cash? Open Day to see pay-outs and notes; check voided bills in Bills.
Only a manager or owner can void a bill. Voided bills stay visible with the reason, so nothing disappears quietly. Give the manager role only to people you trust with cash.
Something not working? Menu → Help on WhatsApp sends us your restaurant and outlet name; we reply the same day.
