Sales playbook
For the Thaliyo team, not for customers

Who we sell to

Cafés and family restaurants in Pune with 5–25 tables, today on paper KOTs, a basic billing PC, or Petpooja. They lose orders between waiter and kitchen, can't match the cash at night, and don't want to pay commission to aggregators for their own customers.

The demo café account

Every demo runs on one demo café, the same way a real restaurant is set up. Creating it is also the best practice run for setup day.

  1. Super Admin → Pipeline → New lead: “Thaliyo Demo Café”, owner = you. On the lead page, Create restaurant → Create and start trial. The owner invite goes to a demo email you control (e.g. demo.owner@…).
  2. Open the invite email, set the password, and finish the Business panel setup: business details, the menu (upload the example spreadsheet: 6–8 categories, 30–40 dishes, a few with sizes and add-ons), tables T1–T10, GST.
  3. Invite three demo staff: waiter, kitchen, cashier (three more emails you control). Set their passwords from the invite emails.
  4. On the demo devices, open the Staff app in Chrome, sign in, and Install / Add to Home screen.
  5. Before each demo: open the day with ₹500, and leave 2 tables running so the screens don't look empty.

What to carry

  • Phone 1: waiter (Staff app, signed in as demo waiter)
  • Phone 2 or small tablet: kitchen screen, sound on
  • Tablet or laptop: POS (signed in as demo cashier)
  • Portable 58 mm printer, paired (if you have one)
  • Mobile hotspot (don't depend on their Wi-Fi)
  • Printed brochure (2–3 copies)
  • Power bank and cables
  • Super Admin open on your phone, to log the lead
Sell what's live. Live today: waiter app, kitchen screen, POS with GST, split bills, printer, offline, day close, sold out, move/merge, Hindi/Marathi, WhatsApp day summary, owner panel and setup. Coming soon (say so if asked): riders app, inventory, owner analytics, customer ordering website.

The 10-minute demo
Hand the owner the waiter phone
TimeDoSay
0:00–1:00Ask two questions: how does an order reach your kitchen today? How do you match cash at night?Listen. Their answers are the pitch.
1:00–3:00Give the owner the waiter phone. They tap a free table, add 2 dishes (one with a size), a note “less spicy”, then Send to kitchen.“Your waiter does exactly this. No slip, no running.”
3:00–4:30The kitchen screen rings. Tap Start, then Ready. Show the waiter phone: the round now says Ready.“The kitchen sees it in seconds, the waiter knows when to pick up.”
4:30–6:30On the POS: open that table, Bill, add a 10% discount, Split 2 (cash + UPI), Settle & print. Show the round-off line and the printed bill.“GST, round-off, split, printed. One tap.”
6:30–7:30Switch off the hotspot. Make a takeaway and bill it. Switch it back on: it syncs.“Internet goes, billing doesn't stop.”
7:30–8:30Day screen: record ₹50 for milk under Pay out, show “Should be in the drawer”. Show Sold out and the language switch (मराठी).“At night you know if cash is short, before anyone goes home.”
8:30–10:00Prices (brochure page 2), the founding offer, and the next step.“Shall we do setup day on [day]? You get 14 days free.”

Close

  • Agree the setup day and who will be there (owner, cashier, one cook, one waiter).
  • Ask for the menu (photo, PDF or Petpooja export), GSTIN and the staff list with emails.
  • Log it in Super Admin: stage Demo done, next follow-up date, expected plan. Send the “After the demo” WhatsApp from the lead page.

Common questions

They sayWe answer
“We already have Petpooja.”We move your menu over free. Same billing, plus the waiter app and kitchen screen in Starter's price range, and day close that matches your cash. Try it 14 days side by side.
“My staff can't use apps.”If they use WhatsApp, they can use this. It's in Marathi and Hindi. We train them on site; most learn it in one shift.
“What if the internet goes?”Billing and orders keep working offline and sync later (you just saw it).
“Is my data safe?”Each restaurant's data is locked to its own account; staff see only their outlet. You can stop anytime.
“Too expensive.”Founding price ₹999/month, locked 12 months. One saved lost order a day pays for it.
“Do you take commission?”Never. Fixed monthly price, 0% commission.
